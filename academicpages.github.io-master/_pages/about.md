I am an Electrical and Computer Engineering Ph.D. candidate at the University of Maryland, College Park. I am part of the Computer Architecture and Systems Lab (CASL), advised by [Dr. Bahar Asgari](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~bahar/).

My research focuses on optimizing resource utilization in sparse applications to improve throughput and latency across CPUs, GPUs, and FPGAs.

Please contact me by email for the latest CV and research overview documents.

## Research Interests

- Computer Architecture
- Domain-Specific Architectures
- Scientific Computing Accelerators
- Generative AI Accelerators
- GPU Architecture
- Heterogeneous Computing
