The Title of Your Most Recent Post, Which Can Run to Two Lines

The deck: one sentence that tells the reader what they get out of this. Not a teaser — an actual summary.

Open with the thing you found, not the background. A reader who already knows the context should get something from the first paragraph, and a reader who doesn't should still know whether to keep going.

Ordinary paragraphs look like this. Inline formatting works as you would expect: bold for the load-bearing claim, italics for emphasis, inline_code() for identifiers, and links in the accent colour.

A section heading

Use h2 for sections and h3 for anything beneath them. Keep the hierarchy honest — screen readers and search engines both read it.

A subsection

Lists come in both flavours. Unordered:

  • One point per item, and keep them parallel in form.
  • If an item needs two sentences, it probably wants to be a paragraph.
  • Three to five items reads best.

And ordered, for anything with a sequence:

  1. First you do this.
  2. Then this.
  3. And the result is the thing you opened with.
